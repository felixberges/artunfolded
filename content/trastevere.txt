	en: Apse mosaic of Santa Maria in Trastevere (Rome), made around 1140–1143 under Pope Innocent II. In the vault, Christ and the Virgin sit on a single throne, surrounded by saints and popes; below are Pietro Cavallini's Stories of the Virgin (1291). The high altar and the distance make it impossible to see in full from the nave. The viewer offers two ways of looking at it: from the floor of the nave, as a visitor sees it, or as a three-dimensional object that can be turned and examined up close from any angle.
